TABUH — Gamelan Defense is a rhythm tower-defense game for the desktop browser:
a Javanese village at dusk is attacked by waves of spirits, and the player defends it
by placing bronze gamelan instruments (Gong, Bonang, Kendang, Saron, Kenong) as towers
along a stone path. Every tower strikes on the beat of a procedural gamelan loop, and
tapping TABUH exactly on the beat builds a combo that multiplies all tower damage.
Survive all 10 nights to save the village. Fully offline, with procedurally drawn art
and synthesized gamelan music (WebAudio).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How to play

- Click a stone pad, then pick an instrument (or press 1–5) to build it. Click a
  built tower to upgrade or sell it.
- Press **Space** (or click the TABUH drum) exactly on the beat: Perfect and Good
  hits raise your combo and boost tower damage; a miss breaks the combo.
- Press **Mulai Malam** to start the next wave. Enemies that reach the village gate
  damage it — if the gate falls, the village is lost.
- **P** pauses, **M** mutes the music, **R** restarts after a win or loss.
